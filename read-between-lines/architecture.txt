ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
=====================

Our platform is composed of several services:

- API Gateway: routes incoming requests to internal services.
- Auth Service: handles authentication and session management.
- Document Service: stores and serves internal documents.
- Notification Service: sends emails and alerts.

Documents are retrieved directly from the server's local filesystem
by the Document Service, based on the filename requested by the
client.

For more details, see the internal architecture wiki.
